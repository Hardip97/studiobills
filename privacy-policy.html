Who We Are

creditmantra is a freelance billing and invoicing web application built and operated by Hardipsinh Zala, an individual developer based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India.

Contact: zalahardip70@gmail.com

What Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your email address and password (managed securely via Supabase Auth)
  • Business data — your studio name, client names, invoice details, income and expense entries that you enter into the app
  • Payment information — for Pro subscribers, payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card number or bank details. Razorpay handles all payment data.
  • Usage data — basic technical data such as browser type and app errors, used only to fix bugs

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data unrelated to billing.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide you access to your billing dashboard
  • Sync your invoices and financial records across devices
  • Process subscription payments via Razorpay
  • Send account-related emails (login, password reset, billing receipts)
  • Improve the app based on general usage patterns

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your billing data is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption.

Supabase servers may be located outside India. By using creditmantra, you consent to your data being stored and processed in these locations.

Learn more about Supabase's security practices at supabase.com/security.

Data Sharing

We share your data only with the following trusted third parties, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider
  • Razorpay — payment processing for Pro subscriptions
  • Vercel — web hosting and content delivery

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any other third parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored about you — export it anytime using the Backup Export feature in the app
  • Correct inaccurate data — edit your profile and entries directly in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data — email us at zalahardip70@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 7 working days
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the service at any time

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 7 working days.

Razorpay may retain payment transaction records as required by Indian financial regulations.

Cookies

creditmantra uses minimal cookies strictly necessary for authentication (to keep you logged in). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

Children's Privacy

creditmantra is intended for use by adults (18+) for business purposes. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.
